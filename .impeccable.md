## Design Context

### Users

City officials, event organizers, residents, journalists, and community groups exploring Toronto road closures, construction, events, and transit impacts.

### Brand Personality

Readable, approachable, practical. The user prefers a light sky-blue theme and rejected the earlier brown palette. Use cool off-white land, sky-blue water and buildings, soft mint parks, and dark blue-grey labels.

### Aesthetic Direction

The user asked to remove the surrounding workspace and add features one at a time. Keep the map full-screen with minimal controls. Carry the light sky-blue direction through both map views and their controls. Keep the overall appearance bright and avoid brown or beige accents.

The user approved a distinct basemap theme and label cleanup first, followed by custom controls later. Hide business and tourist clutter while preserving street, neighbourhood, and transit context. Retain the 3D facades and consistent daylight.

The user requested liquid glass throughout the interface. Use translucent sky-blue surfaces, backdrop blur, softly rounded corners, fine white rim highlights, and subtle SVG edge refraction. Keep text and icons sharp, closure accents coral, and the map canvas unfiltered. Provide opaque fallbacks and respect reduced-transparency and reduced-motion preferences.

The user asked for a clearer, more translucent trial with a rendering library. Small controls use roughly 30% tint and reading panels 52%, with 8–10 px blur. Trial `liquid-glass-react` on the toolbox icon and panel as a decorative layer behind the native controls. Keep the map and drag interactions independent of the optical renderer.

### Design Principles

- Make the map usable before adding surrounding interface.
- Preserve the working worker configuration and map initialization.
- Use colour to distinguish geographic features without implying calculated traffic impacts.
- Keep mode controls labelled, keyboard accessible, and visible over the map.
- Keep future closure and congestion indicators distinguishable from base-map styling.
